# Contracts — Ollama Pack

## OP-MODELFILE-001

Each active model family must have a Modelfile or a documented reason why Ollama is not supported.

## OP-RUNTIME-001

Runtime smoke must record command, endpoint, model, prompt, result, and limitations.

## OP-ARTIFACT-001

Merged models, GGUFs, and runtime exports must stay out of Git.

## OP-HEALTH-001

The track cannot be marked complete until health is estimated at `>= 9.5 / 10`.
